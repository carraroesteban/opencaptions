Hi everyone, and welcome to Horizon Summit. My name is Hiroshi Tanaka, and I work on telehealth for rural clinics.
Today I want to talk about care at a distance, and why the best technology is the one patients barely notice.
Let's start with a story. A few years ago, a nurse in a small town had to drive two hours just to show a test result to a specialist.
So we tried something different. We gave each clinic a simple tablet, a good microphone, and a short training for the whole team.
The first months were hard. The connection dropped, the forms were too long, and some people did not trust the video calls.
We listened, made every step shorter, and added a phone line for anyone without internet.
The result was clear. Waiting times went down, and patients said they felt heard, even through a screen.
My advice is: start small, measure what people feel, and build the service together with the ones who use it.
Thank you very much, and enjoy the rest of the summit.
