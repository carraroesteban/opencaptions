Hi everyone, and welcome to Nerdearla. My name is Alex, and I work as a site reliability engineer.
Today I want to talk about observability in Kubernetes, and why your on-call rotation should not depend on luck.
Let's start with a story. It was three in the morning, the pager went off, and our PostgreSQL primary was running out of connections.
We had dashboards in Grafana, metrics in Prometheus, and logs everywhere, but nobody could answer a simple question: what changed?
So we decided to instrument every service with OpenTelemetry, and to attach the commit and the pull request to every deployment.
The result was simple. When something breaks, the trace tells you which service is slow, and the deployment marker tells you why.
My advice is: start small, measure the things your users feel, like latency and errors, and write runbooks that a tired human can follow.
