# LinkedIn and X

## LinkedIn (English)

> OpenCaptions 1.0 is out: free, open-source live captions and translation for events.
>
> Your audience scans a QR code and reads every talk in their language, on their own phone, a second after it's said. New in 1.0: the speaker's slide translated on every phone, tap any word to have it explained, and one question box for every talk of the event.
>
> About US$0.54 per room-hour with Google's Gemini, or free on a Mac. Try it on your phone: https://try-opencaptions.kvza.ar/s/main
