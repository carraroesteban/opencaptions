# Product Hunt

- **Name:** OpenCaptions
- **Tagline** (60 characters): `Live captions and translation for events, on every phone`
- **Topics:** Accessibility, Open Source, Events, Artificial Intelligence
- **Links:** the website, the live demo, GitHub
- **Gallery** (in this order; the first is the thumbnail): `docs/images/hero.png`; `docs/images/phones.png` (two phones following the same talk in Spanish and English); `docs/images/dashboard.png`; `docs/images/projector.png`; `docs/images/transcript.png`; the 60-second video ([demo-video.md](demo-video.md)). All are screenshots of the real app (`npm run screenshots`), already PNG.

**Description:**

> Your audience scans a QR code and reads every talk live, in their language, on their own phone. Also on the stage screen and in your livestream. New in 1.0: the speaker's slide translated on every phone, tap any word to have it explained, and ask the whole event a question. Free and open source; about US$0.54 per room-hour with Gemini, or free on a Mac.
