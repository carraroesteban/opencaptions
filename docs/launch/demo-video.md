# A 60-second demo video

A real screen recording with sound: the moment that sells it is hearing a speaker and seeing the words land on a phone in another language. No music over the speaker, no generated scenes.

**Record it with what's in the repo:** `npm run demo:record` films the stage screen and a phone while the server captions a sample talk; run it with the talk's audio audible, then record the phone parts below with QuickTime (iPhone mirrored) or the phone's own screen recorder.

| Seconds | Show | Say (voice-over, or as on-screen text) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | A speaker talking in English; the phone beside them shows the Spanish caption arriving | "Every word, on every phone, in its own language." |
| 5–15 | Scanning the QR poster; the room opens; tapping Español | "No app. Scan, pick a language, read." |
| 15–25 | The slide changes on the projector; the phone's Slide sheet shows it translated | "The slide too." |
| 25–35 | Tapping a word in the captions; the explanation | "A word you don't know? Tap it." |
| 35–45 | The transcripts page: a question to the whole event, the answer and the link to the moment | "After the talk, ask the whole event." |
| 45–55 | The dashboard with rooms live; the cost on the report | "One laptop runs every room. About fifty cents an hour, or free on a Mac." |
| 55–60 | The logo and the address | "OpenCaptions. Free and open source." |
