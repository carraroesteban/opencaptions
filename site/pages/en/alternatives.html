OpenCaptions software is free (MIT), with no limit on rooms or attendees; on your own computer, plan on about one room per laptop.

The other options

  • Human captioners (CART): the most accurate and the standard for formal proceedings; book them ahead.
  • Simultaneous interpreters: the best for listening in another language, but one per language pair, with equipment in every room, and no captions.
  • Hosted AI services such as Wordly or Interprefy: convenient, with support, priced by plan or usage. Check their prices and where they process your audio.
  • Meeting tools (Zoom, Teams, Meet): caption their own calls, not an audience in a room reading on their phones.

When to choose something else

  • Every word must be exact, as in courts, hearings or medical settings: book a human captioner, or have someone review the AI transcripts.
  • Your audience needs sign language: captions don't replace sign language interpreters.
  • Nobody on your team can set up a computer with the sound desk and you'd rather have a vendor handle everything: a managed service may be worth its price.

For everything else (conferences, classes, public meetings, festivals, places of worship), OpenCaptions gives every attendee captions in their language for the cost of the AI, or for free.
